# UI audit, Nov 10

A senior design pass over every screen at 1440 and 390 wide, light and dark: flow, balance, whitespace, alignment
and the small details. Fixed in four passes (shell, clients, tasks, templates and portal); each item says where.

## Data and flow
- **The sample had gone stale.** Its dates were fixed in late October, so by Nov 10 eight tasks read "Overdue", the
  calendar's current week was empty and "Due soon" said 8. Seed dates are now relative to today, so the sample
  always looks like a live week. *(Shell pass)*
- **Page headers didn't match.** Clients put its buttons under the description, on the left; Tasks put its
  button on the right; Templates had none. One header now: eyebrow, title, description, actions on the right
  (under it on phones). *(Shell pass)*
- **Eyebrows repeated the left bar.** "Client management" sat over the page title and as the sidebar heading.
  The eyebrow now says something useful (counts, the client's industry, the saved view). *(Shell pass)*

## Shell
- The details panel's first line sat 4px higher than the page's; both now start on the same line.
- "Sample" beside each client in the left bar truncated names ("Fieldnotes Jou…"); the sample label stays on the
  client's page and cards, not in the nav.
- The sidebar heading "Client management" duplicated the product name; it's "Workspace".

## Clients
- **Board:** only three of five stages were visible beside the details panel, with nothing saying there were more.
  Columns are narrower, the row scrolls with snap, and an edge fade shows there is more.
- **Board:** column headers sat 8px left of the cards under them. Headers and cards share one inset.
- **List:** stage badges were right-aligned at different widths, so the column was ragged; the next milestone
  wrapped to two lines. Fixed-width stage column, one-line milestone.
- **Table:** client names wrapped to two lines and the last column was cut off. Names stay on one line, the long
  "What they do" column shows only on wide screens, and the milestone truncates.
- **Client overview:** links were 44px apart, people 32px; one rhythm for both.

## Tasks
- **Phone list:** the title was squeezed into a one-word column beside the meta. On phones the meta now drops
  to a second line under the title.
- **Toolbar:** on phones the search box ran off the screen. Search takes its own full-width row on phones; the
  view switcher scrolls if it must.
- **Filter panel:** rows were unevenly spaced (assignee rows stretched to match the client list). Three columns,
  each packed to the top, one row height.
- **Table:** full-size date fields dominated every row and "JAM Studio" wrapped. Compact date fields, assignee on
  one line.
- **Calendar:** weekday labels and day numbers used different insets; aligned.

## Templates and portal
- **Template covers** were Between Gigs' old dark-green screenshots, a different brand from the app around them.
  Each template now has a Swiss Grid cover drawn from its own phases.
- **Portal header on phones:** "JAM Studio" and "Back to client management" each wrapped to two lines. Compact
  mark and a short "Back" on phones; a heavy rule under the header like the app's.
- **Portal "Waiting on you":** a long title pushed its button below, so rows didn't match. Button on the right on
  wider screens, under the title on phones, the same for every row.
